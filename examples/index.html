@pmndrs/upscaler — Examples

FSR1 spatial + FSR2/3-style temporal upscaling — WebGPU, Three.js and Babylon.js.

WebGPU-only — open in Chrome/Edge 113+ (or a browser with WebGPU enabled). Each demo renders the scene at a lower resolution and upscales it back to full size.

Three.js Babylon.js Raw WebGPU

Three.js

16 examples and 4 showcases using the Three.js WebGPURenderer.

Showcases

What the upscaler buys you, made visible — bigger scenes built to be looked at rather than to test one feature.

S1
Reinvest the savings
One GPU budget, two ways to spend it: render a quarter of the pixels and buy SSGI + SSR with the difference, or render every pixel plain. A mouse wipe compares them; each side's GPU milliseconds are measured live with timestamp queries, and auto-balance finds the render scale where the effects fit native's budget.
budget
S2
Raymarched fractal
A Mandelbox flight where every pixel runs a long raymarch — a 2–3× render scale turns a slideshow into a smooth framerate. No meshes: the raymarcher supplies its own depth and motion vectors.
per-pixel cost
S3
How low can you go
One slider drags the render resolution down to 1/8 per axis — 1.6% of the pixels. FSR vs bilinear from the same render, with a pixel loupe.
stunt
S4
Watch it converge
Step the real temporal pipeline one frame at a time: the jittered low-res input, every debug buffer, the Halton jitter filling the pixel, and a magnifier where detail appears frame by frame.
explainer
